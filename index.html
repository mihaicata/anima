This app remaps your camera feed to approximate how a carrion crow's tetrachromatic, UV‑sensitive, motion‑tuned eyes process the same scene.

Why it looks like this
Fourth coneCrows are tetrachromats — a UV‑sensitive cone lets them see near‑ultraviolet, rendered here as violet bloom.
Muted redCorvid colour space leans blue/violet‑forward with weaker long‑wavelength (red) response, so warm tones are pulled down.
Faster flicker fusionCrows resolve motion at a higher rate than we do — moving edges are highlighted in cyan as things a crow would catch that we might miss.
Wider fieldLaterally set eyes give near‑panoramic view. The gentle edge‑darkening hints at that broader periphery.
Video is processed on‑device only — nothing is uploaded or stored.
